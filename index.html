About

I believe truly great design leads to business growth because of how we solve our customers' real problems; critically, this includes understanding the problem behind what our customers are telling us, shipping software people actually want to use, and using both qualitative and quantitative evidence to inform and measure these decisions.

I believe good design work leads to a healthy tension. Advocating for our customers is not always a smooth process.

Before design, I got my start working in the trade publishing industry for various large publishers, including Penguin Random House, as a managing editor of physical and digital products.
